Steel Bridge

As a 2025 Pacific Southwest Symposium representative, I built and competed with a 200+ lb steel bridge and proposed a transportation project — competing on a national level.

FIG. 01 — STEEL BRIDGE

Overview

The steel bridge competition is hands-on structural engineering: a full-scale bridge that must be fabricated, transported, and assembled against the clock, then load-tested. Building it developed real shop skills — welding, grinding, and metal cutting — alongside the structural design work.

At the March 2025 Pacific Southwest Symposium I also proposed a transportation project, placing 7th for Transportation at the national-level event.
