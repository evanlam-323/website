Stair-Climbing Robot

I fabricated a precise arm mechanism to assist in navigating the 45° incline while carrying a fragile payload — securing the 2nd-fastest time in 10 years at the biannual UH Mechanical Engineering Competition.

FIG. 01 — STAIR CLIMBER

Overview

The challenge: climb a steep 45° incline quickly without damaging a fragile payload. That put two requirements in tension — enough torque and traction to climb fast, and enough stability that the payload survived the ride.

As a team member, I designed and fabricated a precise arm mechanism — an egg-collection lever — in CAD, then 3D-printed and iterated it to reliably pick up and secure the fragile egg payload while the robot climbed the 45° incline. The team's build earned the 2nd-fastest time in 10 years of the competition.
