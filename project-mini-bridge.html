3D-Printed Mini-Bridge

As Captain, I led a multidisciplinary team of 4 in the design, fabrication, and testing of a 3D-printed mini-bridge capable of supporting over 70 lbs.

FIG. 01 — BRIDGE ASSEMBLY

Overview

The project challenges a team to design a lightweight 3D-printed structure that carries a large load relative to its own weight. Beyond the engineering, it is a coordination exercise: keeping Civil/Structural and Mechanical Engineering members aligned on schedule, competition rules, and design specifications.

I coordinated schedules and facilitated communication across disciplines to ensure the build stayed compliant with competition requirements while hitting the 70+ lb load target.
