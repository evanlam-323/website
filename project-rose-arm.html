Robotics Space Exploration (RoSE) / 2024 — Present

Team RoSE 6 DoF Arm

As Mechanical Lead for UH Mānoa's Robotics Space Exploration team, I spearheaded the design of a 6-DOF robotic arm with full radial accessibility, featuring a differential-drive wrist powered by advanced 3-phase motor systems.

FIG. 01 — 6-DOF ARM

Overview

The arm was designed for the University Rover Challenge, where a rover must perform dexterous tasks at range — actuating switches, picking up objects, and typing. Full radial accessibility lets the end effector reach any orientation around the target, and the differential-drive wrist packs two axes of motion into a compact, high-stiffness joint.

The team placed 24th out of 114 teams globally at the 2025 University Rover Competition.
