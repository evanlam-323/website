Additive Pump Subsystem

As an Undergraduate Research Assistant, I'm designing a pump subsystem to be incorporated into a custom additive-manufacturing platform optimized for elastic and compliant materials.

FIG. 01 — PUMP SUBSYSTEM

Overview

Printing elastic and compliant materials is hard: the feedstock resists consistent, controlled extrusion. This platform targets that problem, and the pump subsystem is the part that meters and drives material to the nozzle reliably.

I collaborate with a team of 5 to integrate the material pump with the rest of the print head — static mixers, heating elements, and the extrusion path — so elastic and compliant material is conditioned and delivered for a clean, repeatable build.
