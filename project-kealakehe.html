As a fabricator and designer on the FIRST robotics team, I contributed 10+ hours weekly to team projects, mentorship, and competitive engineering development across three seasons.

FIG. 01 — COMPETITION ROBOT

Overview

FIRST is a fast, iterative build season: design, fabricate, test, and compete a robot against a fresh game every year. Three seasons of it built the foundation for everything I do now — hands-on fabrication, working in a team, and iterating under a deadline.

Across three seasons (2021 — 2024) at 10+ hours a week, the team earned World Championship qualification (top <10%) and finished 18th in division.
