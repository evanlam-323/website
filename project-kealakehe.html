Overview

FIRST is a fast, iterative build season: design, fabricate, test, and compete a robot against a fresh game every year. Three seasons of it built the foundation for everything I do now — hands-on fabrication, working in a team, and iterating under a deadline.

The team earned World Championship qualification (top <10%) and finished 18th in division.
